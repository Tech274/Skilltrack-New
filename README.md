# Skill Navigator

SkillTrack-365 - AI-Powered Certification, Skills & Career Intelligence Platform

Build a world-class AI-powered SaaS platform called SkillTrack-365 that helps learners discover, compare, prepare for, pass, and advance through professional certifications while enabling enterprises to manage workforce skills, certifications, compliance, and career growth.

Platform Tagline

Track Skills. Predict Success. Accelerate Careers.

Platform Positioning

SkillTrack 365 is not a training portal.

It is a complete Certification Intelligence, Career Growth, Skills Development, and Workforce Readiness Platform.

The platform combines:

Certification Marketplace

AI Career Advisor

Learners

Discover certifications, prepare for exams, track progress, and grow careers.
